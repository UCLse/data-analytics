# Authorship, attribution and public-release boundary

## 1. Author and research origin

**Sayara Beg** is the course author and the originator and research lead of Heat Cascade.

Lecturer, UCL Centre for Systems Engineering, Department of Space and Climate Physics, Faculty of Mathematical and Physical Sciences.

[UCL profile](https://profiles.ucl.ac.uk/106848-sayara-beg)

This is a UCL teaching activity for the Centre for Systems Engineering within the Department of Space and Climate Physics. The UCL logo identifies the university and remains its institutional brand asset; course licence notices do not grant rights in that logo.

Heat Cascade is currently an unfunded research project. This public teaching release introduces general Python analytics using entirely synthetic observations and invented parameters. Detailed proposal architecture, work-package plans and the funding strategy are withheld.

## 2. Required credit

Reused or adapted course text, code, datasets, figures and derivative sites must preserve the Sayara Beg author notice and acknowledge the course source. Publicly presented analyses using these teaching materials should cite the course and identify modifications.

Recommended citation:

Beg, S. (2026). Heat Cascade: Python data analytics teaching course (version 1.1). https://uclse.github.io/data-analytics/.

Attribution must be visible to the reader or user, rather than confined to an inaccessible private record. The UCL affiliation identifies the author; it does not grant an institutional endorsement of an adaptation.

## 3. Licence and research boundary

The repository's existing GPL-3.0 licence remains applicable to covered materials. Author notices must be preserved with redistributed material under the applicable licence. The citation statement sets the academic attribution expectation; it does not revoke rights already granted for earlier releases or restrict rights conferred by the licence.

The public release supplies educational examples. It does not disclose the private research proposal or authorise claims that another person originated Sayara Beg's research. Attribution notices cannot, by themselves, prevent independent research on a similar idea. The specific proposal remains outside this teaching release.
