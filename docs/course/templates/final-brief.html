1. Decision question and system boundary

State the decision question, intended user and analytical output. Define the geographical, temporal and observation boundaries. Explain the connection between ecosystem condition, heat, infrastructure and community vulnerability relevant to this question.

2. Data preparation and assurance

State that all supplied data are synthetic. Identify the source files and keys. Report cleaning consequences, join cardinality, missingness and the denominator used for each reported rate.

3. Analysis and comparator

Explain why the method addresses the question. Define the outcome and inputs. Specify the baseline and test boundary for prediction, or the objective, feasible options and assumptions for scenarios. Report the minimum figures and tables needed to assess the result.

4. Independent extension and decision implications

Describe an extension beyond the guided code. Explain its purpose and result. State how the extension changes, qualifies or leaves the decision conclusion unchanged.

5. Conclusion

Give a conditional recommendation tied to the declared boundary. Identify the observation or change that would trigger review.

5.1. Contribution, limitations and further work

Explain the contribution to the stated analytical question. Distinguish exploratory patterns from causal effects and scenario assumptions from observations. Identify measurement, construct or validation gaps and propose a practical next assessment.

6. Reproducibility record

Provide package versions, input filenames, row counts and instructions for reproducing the outputs. Follow the approved requirements for references and disclosure of assistance.
