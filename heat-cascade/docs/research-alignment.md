# Research context and public teaching boundary

## 1. Research origin

Heat Cascade originates from research led by **Sayara Beg**, Lecturer, UCL Centre for Systems Engineering, Department of Space and Climate Physics, Faculty of Mathematical and Physical Sciences. [UCL profile](https://profiles.ucl.ac.uk/106848-sayara-beg). Funding has not yet been secured.

The public course introduces environmental data analysis at a general educational level. Fictional neighbourhoods connect canopy, temperature, aggregate service demand and recorded decisions. These connections support teaching questions; they do not publish the project's detailed research design.

## 2. What the teaching release contains

The workshops introduce data preparation, exploratory analysis, simple models and comparison under uncertainty. Each exercise explains its observation boundary, assumptions and interpretation requirements. The supplied records and parameters are entirely synthetic.

The teaching index, scenario calculator and questionnaire scores are invented exercises. Their outputs do not constitute empirical findings, validated research instruments or operational advice.

## 3. What remains outside the public release

Detailed proposal architecture, proposed work packages, planned research integrations, unpublished methods and funding strategy remain private. The public course should not be used to infer that a proposed research capability has been funded, implemented or validated.

The [real-data readiness template](../templates/real-data-readiness.md) addresses generic analytical checks. It is a teaching aid, rather than a specification of Heat Cascade's private research programme.
